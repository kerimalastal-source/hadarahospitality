Based in Istanbul, Türkiye, we help hospitality teams source hotel textiles and guest essentials suited to their property and their guests.

WHO WE ARE

Built around the needs
of hospitality.

HADARA Hospitality is an Istanbul-based company and part of BYHADARA Group. We focus on the procurement and supply of hotel textiles and guest essentials for 3 to 5-star hotels, resorts and serviced apartments.

Working with Turkish manufacturing partners, we help clients explore products, agree on specifications, review samples and coordinate supply for their projects.

OUR APPROACH

From requirements
to delivery.

Every property has its own standards and operating needs. We begin with the details: product type, materials, sizes, quantities, branding and delivery requirements. From there, we coordinate options and samples with manufacturing partners before commercial terms and production are confirmed.
