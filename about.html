WHO WE ARE

Hospitality supply

At HADARA Hospitality, we are a Turkish hospitality supply company headquartered in Istanbul and part of the BYHADARA Group, active in the hospitality and tourism investment sector.

We specialize in supplying hotel linen, towels, robes, slippers, and guest room amenities tailored for 3 to 5-star hotels, helping hotels maintain consistent hospitality standards and guest comfort.

Our carefully selected product collections are designed to meet the operational needs and hospitality standards of modern hotels and resorts.
