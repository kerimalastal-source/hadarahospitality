Private Labeling & Custom Branding for Hotel Textiles

September 10, 2026 · 3 min read · HADARA Hospitality Team

Custom branding on hotel textiles — an embroidered logo on a robe, a woven monogram on a towel border, a labeled amenity bottle — is one of the lowest-cost ways to make a generic product feel like it belongs to your property specifically.

What’s usually possible

  • Embroidered or woven logos on robes, towels and bedding
  • Custom labeling on amenity bottles and guest room consumables
  • Property-specific packaging for welcome and gift sets
  • Color and trim customization within a manufacturer’s standard range

Plan branding into the production timeline, not after it

Custom branding adds a production step, so it needs to be part of the sampling and approval stage — not requested after a standard order has already been confirmed. Building it in early keeps lead times predictable and avoids a rushed, lower-quality result.
